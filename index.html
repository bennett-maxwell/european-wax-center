Franchise Opportunity Profile

European Wax Center

European Wax Center is a waxing franchise offered by EWC Franchisor LLC since 2006. Every fact on this page is sourced from the EWC Franchisor LLC 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (issued April 22, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$45,000

New franchisees; $36,000 for existing franchisees. 50% refundable only if we terminate for your failure to perform certain pre-opening obligations. (FDD Item 5, pp.11-12)

Estimated Initial Investment

$327,600 to $836,950

$63,000-$66,350 of that is payable to the franchisor or affiliates (new franchisees). Development fee $72,000-$207,000 for 3+ centers. (FDD cover, p.1; Item 7, pp.21-22)

Royalty

6% of Gross Sales

Payable weekly. Cure-period uplift to 150%/200% if successor-term requirements are completed late. (FDD Item 6, pp.14, 19)

Marketing Fund

3% of Gross Sales

Payable monthly to affiliate EWC MFund, LLC. (FDD Item 6, p.14)

Territory

Protected, not exclusive

Protected territory granted after lease acceptance; no new EWC franchises inside it, but franchisor reserves e-commerce, alternate channels, and acquisition rights. (FDD Item 12, pp.48-50)

Term

10 years + one 10-year successor

Successor fee $5,000; disputes in Collin County, Texas under Texas law. (FDD Item 17, pp.56, 60)

Facility

1,200 to 1,600 sq ft

Leased retail space with 5 to 7 waxing suites (min 5, max 7 without approval). (FDD Item 1, p.2; Item 5, p.12)

Training

Brand Immersion + Core Training

90-day center-manager program plus ~6-day launch training; no charge for standard initial training. (FDD Item 11, pp.43-44)

Time to Open

120 days

The Franchised Center must open within 120 days of signing the Franchise Agreement. (FDD Item 11, p.43)

Owner Model

Dedicated center manager

Center must be under direct, full-time, daily supervision of a trained dedicated center manager. 5%+ owners sign an unlimited guaranty. (FDD Item 15, p.55)

HQ

Plano, Texas

EWC Franchisor LLC, Granite Park V, 5830 Granite Parkway, Suite 300, Plano, TX 75024. (469) 270-6500. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

1,067 outlets

1,062 franchised + 5 company-owned as of 1/4/2025; +25.2% franchised outlets 2022-2024; zero terminations/non-renewals 2022-2024. (FDD Item 20, p.68)

Item 19 — Financial Performance

The franchisor makes no financial performance representations about the future financial performance of franchisees or the past financial performance of company-owned or franchised outlets, and does not authorize its employees or representatives to make any such representations orally or in writing.

This means the FDD contains no average revenues, margins, or earnings figures for European Wax Center outlets. If you are purchasing an existing center, the franchisor may provide that center's actual records. Any other financial performance information you receive should be reported to the franchisor's management team, the FTC, and the appropriate state regulatory agencies. (FDD Item 19, p.67.)

Risk Profile & Disclosure

44 / 100 risk score

Assessment: Moderate. A large, still-growing system (848 to 1,062 franchised outlets in three years with zero terminations) weighed against a high investment ceiling of $836,950, a protected-but-not-exclusive territory with franchisor e-commerce competition, four prior litigation matters plus a Washington AG no-poaching investigation, a multi-layer securitization ownership structure, and no Item 19 earnings data at all.
